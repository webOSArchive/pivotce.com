# News Crumbs – 29th June 2014

The toaster’s been getting pretty hot recently, but still every slice is browned to perfection! Time to sweep out some more crumbs…

- Matias Duarte has had a couple of ego boosts:
  [Why do all mobile roads lead back to the Palm Pre?](http://thetechblock.com/mobile-roads-lead-back-palm-pre/).
  [I’ve Discovered The Best Smartphone Of All Time, And It’s From 2011](http://www.forbes.com/sites/ianmorris/2014/06/16/ive-discovered-the-best-smartphone-of-all-time-and-its-from-2011).
  Discuss here: [Matias Duarte: “Wow, it’s WebOS nostalgia week!”](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-discussion-lounge/328094-matias-duarte-wow-its-webos-nostalgia-week.html) and here: [Wow Forbes (still!) loves the Palm Pre and webOS](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-discussion-lounge/328077-wow-forbes-still-loves-palm-pre-webos.html).
- On the TV front, LG has released a [software development kit](http://forums.webosnation.com/lg-webos-tv/328117-lg-rolls-out-webos-sdk-lg-smart-tvs.html).
- Want to start simply? Roy Sutton has made a [short video](http://developer.lge.com/webostv/develop/web-app/how/basic-html-app) (<5mins) on how you can make a simple app with just HTML. It should work on other webOS devices too. [I’m going to try it!](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-development/328151-creating-basic-html-app.html)
- Having sold a million, it seems LG is going [all in on webOS TVs](http://www.wirelessweek.com/news/2014/06/lg-readies-updated-webos-smart-tv-platform-meet-android-tv-head). There has been mention of other webOS domestic devices and possibly wearables.
- You’ll have seen the warning in the previous posting. It’s [now fixed](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-patches/327662-patch-google-sync-https-fix-unknown-error-sign.html#post3416720) of course with help from [frantid](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-patches/327662-patch-google-sync-https-fix-unknown-error-sign-17.html#post3421130) and others. There are rumours that [Grabber5.0](http://pivotce.com/2014/01/31/dev-highlight-grabber5-0/) is installing a flux-capacitor to his Maverick to patch issues *before* they even occur.
- Even as Grabber5.0 keeps our old stuff running, [Garfonso](http://pivotce.com/2013/11/15/dev-highlight-garfonso/) has just released [the future of synergy](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-development/328133-c-dav-synergy-connector-owncloud-google-yahoo-icloud.html) for general testing. The C+DAV connector was built for the [Open webOS port](http://webos-ports.org/wiki/C%2B_Dav_Synergy_Connector), but also works on legacy webOS. It will install as a separate account and for safety, only syncs one-way at the moment. It syncs the calendar and contacts apps with owncloud, google, yahoo and iCloud.

Phew, after all that, I need a toasted bagel!
