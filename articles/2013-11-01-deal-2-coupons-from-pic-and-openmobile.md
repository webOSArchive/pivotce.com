# Deal – 2 Coupons from PIC and OpenMobile

It looks like [OpenMobile](www.openmobileww.com) is offering a $5 off code on their Twitter and [Phoenix International Communications](http://phxdevices.com) is offering a $10 off code on their [store](http://store.phxdevices.com/index.php?id_product=8&controller=product) for entering your email address. Unfortunately, you can’t combine them but if you buy two licenses seperately (add one to cart, checkout, repeat) you can save $15!

> [#ACLforwebos](https://twitter.com/search?q=%23ACLforwebos&src=hash) will be here before you know it! Pre-order & use code ACL5T for $5 off! Apps on [#webOS](https://twitter.com/search?q=%23webOS&src=hash)… here we come! <http://t.co/qA114I7IGA>
>
> — OpenMobile (@openmobileww) [November 1, 2013](https://twitter.com/openmobileww/statuses/396257963801071616)

You can also refresh the Phoenix Store to enter your email address for another $10 off code but like I said, you can’t combine them.  So head on over and get your discounts.

To all those pre-orderers of ACL for webOS, OpenMobile sent emails awarding a $10 off code for their OpenMobile AppMall.  There is some [speculation](http://forums.webosnation.com/hp-touchpad/324645-pic-kick-starts-touchpad-open-mobile-acl-71.html#post3405995) to the legality of the AppMall but as of yet, there seems to be no hard evidence to support the validity of these claims.  OpenMobile [claims](http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/openmobile-world-wide-launches-appmall-185871182.html) they have “partnered with highly reputable content companies and distributors in the world to deliver premium content as well as the best free applications”.
