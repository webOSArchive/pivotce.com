# webOS-Patches Web Portal hacked

**UPDATE:  Turns out it was a bot that didn’t even get root but took php.**

In case you are wondering why you can’t reach [patches.webos-internals.org](http://patches.webos-internals.org/) today…

According to their official Twitter account they got and hacked and were forced to shutdown the portal:

> [@MaverickMatt71](https://twitter.com/MaverickMatt71) it will not – it got hacked, and we needed to nuke it, and we have no one to rewrite it to make it safe.
>
> — WebOS Internals (@webosinternals) [December 22, 2013](https://twitter.com/webosinternals/statuses/414655371144216576)
