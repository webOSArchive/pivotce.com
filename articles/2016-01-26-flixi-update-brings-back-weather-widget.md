# Flixi Update Brings Back Weather Widget

Still rocking that HP TouchPad with webOS? Me too. Love exhibition mode? I bet. Good news! [Under Cloud Games](https://twitter.com/underclouds) developer (aka webOS Forums user, QILLER) has released an update to [Flixi](http://www.webosnation.com/flixi).

[Flixi](http://www.webosnation.com/quick-app-flixi) is an exhibition mode app that combines the best of multitasking and widgets into a nice looking and customizable interface.

The latest update to Flixi includes a repair of the weather widget.

> Okay, weather widget is now working again in the version posted to <https://t.co/SCXd8Olzz1>
>
> — Under Clouds Studio (@underclouds) [January 26, 2016](https://twitter.com/underclouds/status/691837944433397760)

Try it out and don’t forget to say thanks to [Under Clouds Studio](http://www.underclouds.com) for the update! You can get Flixi [from Preware](http://preware.pivotce.com/package/com.underclouds.flixi).
