# Merry Christmas and Happy New Year from pivotCE!

2013 is almost over and what a year for webOS it has been!  Ok, so it’s been pretty sleepy around here, but there are many things that webOS users have been “given” over the years that we are certainly still using.  In fact, these things are what make being a webOS user still fun, easy, and relevant today.  So, for the next 12 days…let’s call it the “12 Days of webOS-mas”…we’ll be paying homage to 12 of the most important technologies, apps, utilities, and functions of webOS.  The first one comes tomorrow so after you wake up from your “afternoon-gift-coma-nap” head on over and check it out.

The team of pivotCE wants to thank all of our donors, contributors, and of course our readers for the last several months and we wish you and your families a Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year!

We are just three months old and are overwhelmed with the support and positive feedback we continue to receive.  I hope we can continue this project with the same enthusiasm as now.  We can’t do it without your support!

The webOS excitement starts in January at [CES and the first LG webOS TV’s](http://pivotce.com/2013/12/20/lg-will-show-webos-tvs-at-ces-2014/).  We can’t wait to see what 2014 will bring to the webOS universe!
