# News Crumbs – 15th June 2014.

Here are some news crumbs we found when cleaning out the webOS toaster:

- [LG sells One million webOS TVs](http://www.theverge.com/2014/6/2/5771428/lg-webos-tv-one-million-sales-announcement)
- Twitter client, [Project Macaw](http://pivotce.com/2014/01/01/the-eighth-day-of-webos-mas-project-macaw/) recently got an incremental update to 1.5.3. It’s available in Preware, but you will need to have added the feed: http://www.minego.net/preware
- [Derek Kessler podcasts on the legacy of webOS](http://www.imore.com/vector-44-legacy-webos) (but not on [webOS Nation](http://forums.webosnation.com/general-news-discussion/328045-rene-ritchie-derek-kessler-podcast-webos.html)).
- <http://www.webOS-internals.org> caused a [minor panic](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-internals/328051-webos-internals-org-not-working.html) this week when it went off-line due to domain registration problems, but it’s now returned. Relax!
