# Ready for LuneOS

We just made our [Preware Catalog](https://pivotce.com/2015/04/17/bye-bye-app-catalog-hello-preware-catalog/) and [pivotCE Preware Feed](https://pivotce.com/2015/04/22/new-preware-and-our-own-feed/) ready for LuneOS.

The Preware Catalog got a new field “Compatibility”  which shows you in an Apple like way with which webOS versions an app/patch is [compatible or not](http://preware.pivotce.com/package/com.palm.org.e-lnx.wee.apps.modeswitcher), or wether it even [requires LuneOS](http://preware.pivotce.com/package/de.pattyland.telegram-webos).

When you add a package to our feed you can now set webOS 3.5 aka LuneOS as a requirement or as a not supported version.

But you are not a developer you say? That’s not a requirement to help! Test and add all apps you find to our feed to fill Preware with life. Do know not where to start? Take [this list](http://forums.webosnation.com/3427741-post58.html) as an example!
