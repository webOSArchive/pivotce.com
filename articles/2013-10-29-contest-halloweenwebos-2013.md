# Contest – Halloween/webOS 2013

## Update: This contest is over! Stay tuned while we pick a winner…

It’s time for Halloween and our first contest here at pivotCE! ![Alan Morford's pivotCE Pumpkin](../images/files/2013/10/CIMG0069.jpg)

The rules are easy: Make something about Halloween and webOS!  Take a picture and send it to [halloween@pivotce.com](mailto:halloween@pivotce.com) or put it on Twitter with [#webOSzombie](https://twitter.com/search?q=%23WebOSZombie&src=hash)!

We will put our favorites here on pivotce.com and pick a winner of a $10 Gift Card for Google Play, the biggest source of apps for your TouchPad with ACL for webOS!
