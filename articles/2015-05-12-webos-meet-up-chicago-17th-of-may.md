# webOS meet up: Chicago, 17th of May.

The next Chicago webOS Meetup will be held on Sunday May 17th at [Moretti’s Ristorante and Pizzeria](http://www.morettischicago.com/schaumburg/index.php) in Schaumburg 1893 Walden Office Square, Schaumburg, IL 60173.

As usual, there will be much to discuss. This is the first meet up since the HP profile & app catalog servers were shut down. There are backups to be made, apps to be saved, YouTube to be fixed!

You can stay up to date by following the twitter account: [@chiwebosmeetup](https://twitter.com/@chiwebosmeetup).

This is the dedicated [bigtent](http://www.bigtent.com/group/chiwebos) page.

Here is the [forum thread](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-events/313031-chicago-webos-meetup-4.html#post3437395).

The team at pivotCE remind you that if you are planning a meet up, let us know! We are interested in promoting your events (it might even increase attendance!). We are also interested in reports and pictures from webOS events.
