# webOS meetup, Chicago, 22nd November

Yes, the Chicago webOS meetup remains a calendar fixture and it’s happening on this coming Sunday, 22nd November, 7pm at [Moretti’s Ristorante and Pizzeria](http://www.morettischicago.com/schaumburg/index.php), Schamburg.

I can only imagine what subjects will be covered, but I can’t imagine how they will fit it all in!

- There’s the latest [release of LuneOS](https://pivotce.com/2015/10/07/luneos-october-stable-release-caffe-latte/).
- New apps are appearing (here’s one).
- LuneOS on [more devices](https://pivotce.com/2015/10/16/luneos-on-more-devices/).
- There are the parts for a complete fix of the certificate problem: [OpenSSL Updater](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-internals/330666-openssl-updater-fixing-certificate-issues.html) & [a root certificate update](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-apps-games/330689-root-cert-updater-app.html).
- Not too mention all the other tips, tricks and fixes for webOS in 2015.

Find the details and Sign up [here](http://www.bigtent.com/group/chiwebos) or comment on the [webOS Nation thread](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-events/313031-chicago-webos-meetup-4.html#post3443239).
[Click here for your directions](http://www.morettischicago.com/schaumburg/directions.php).

The team at pivotCE remind you that if you are planning a meet up, let us know! We are interested in promoting your events (it might even increase attendance!). We are also interested in reports and pictures from webOS events.
