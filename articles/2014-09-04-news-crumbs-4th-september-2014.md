# News Crumbs – 4th September 2014

There’s been a lot of big news in the last week, so time for a quick clean out!

- Some app updates went through HP’s admin last week. We know there are a couple more in the pipeline. Thanks to all the developers still supporting webOS. Expect reviews soon on pivotCE.
- Platform game, [Orcrest](http://developer.palm.com/appredirect/?packageid=com.badillo.rivera.app.orcrest) has new levels. See the pivotCE article [here](http://pivotce.com/2013/12/15/orcrest/).
- [What’s Shaking](https://developer.palm.com/appredirect/?packageid=com.kliktrayllc.whatsshaking) is a must for earthquake… er fans?
- [Zap Photo Share](https://developer.palm.com/appredirect/?packageid=com.hominidsoftware.zapphotoshare) let’s you share photos and slideshows across the wifi network. Why hand your phone around when you can beam to a bigger screen?
- [Lithium Messenger](https://developer.palm.com/appredirect/?packageid=com.scienceapps.lithium) is an assistant for messaging, posting & tweeting. Expand words, add emoticons, pics, videos, shorten links, even translate! Is there a news crumbs mode? Maybe in the next update.
- LG posted photos of [Dev camp 2014](http://developer.lge.com/webOSTV/community/news/). Owners of webOS TVs eagerly await the results of their efforts.
- Meanwhile we were concerned to hear that two of the top designers had [moved to Pebble](http://www.theverge.com/2014/8/18/6031281/the-team-behind-webos-tv-jumps-to-pebble-to-design-the-next). Will this affect the progress of webOS? We hope not.
- Further disappointment came from [This article from Gigaom.com](http://gigaom.com/2014/08/28/a-failed-experiment-how-lg-screwed-up-its-webos-acquisition/), claiming that all is not well in [LG’s Sillicon Valley Lab](http://lgsvl.com/). Some disputed the fairness on twitter and [webOS Nation](http://forums.webosnation.com/lg-webos-tv/328445-interesting-read-webos-team-lg.html). Have a read and decide for yourself.

I hope you enjoyed that, I fancy a nice [grilled pannini](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panini_(sandwich)) to go with my [affogato](http://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Cookbook:Affogato).
