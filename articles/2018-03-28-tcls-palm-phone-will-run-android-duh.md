# TCL’s Palm phone will run android…duh

![](../images/files/2018/03/Palm_logo_2005.png)

Android Police broke the story earlier today that [TCL will launch a Palm branded phone via Verizon](https://www.androidpolice.com/2018/03/28/not-2009-verizon-planning-launch-palm-smartphone-later-year/) sometime in the 2nd quarter this year.  And surprise…it runs Android.

Sadly that’s all we know for now.

It’s taken TCL a long time to do anything with the Palm brand.  We first talked about it [3 years ago](https://pivotce.com/2015/01/07/tcl-relaunches-the-palm-brand/). It started as an unveil of sorts at CES in 2015 when [they announced](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/tcl-communication-co-recreates-palm-300016924.html) they had purchased the Palm brand and they’d be crowd-sourcing from “Palm’s very own community” what to do with it.  No idea what they meant by that… They even launched a website placeholder that directed palm.com to mynewpalm.com. Both of which are no longer doing anything. But trust me when I say I’ll be hammering the refresh button for the near future on palm.com.

Fun fact:  This topic was also the [fourth to last article](https://www.webosnation.com/its-confirmed-tcl-bringing-back-palm) written on the now silent webOSNation.com to give you a reference for how long it took TCL to do something!

At any rate, I’m glad the wheels are turning and I’ll hold judgement until I see what they release though the carrier exclusivity announcement makes my eye twitch. But I agree with Mr. Bohn.

> The fact that TCL is partnering with Verizon to make the new Palm-branded phone is too much.
>
> Literally the carrier that put the dagger in the heart of webOS by pulling support is going to get a nostalgia bait money grab device likely filled with bloat<https://t.co/R5QOz656tU>
>
> — Dieter Bohn (@backlon) [March 28, 2018](https://twitter.com/backlon/status/979129338103820288?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw)

Even former Palm CEO Jon Rubinstein knew [carrier exclusivity aided in the death of webOS](http://bgr.com/2013/06/12/palm-ceo-rubinstein-interview-hp/) and the remnants of Palm. “*I think the deal we had with Verizon really hurt us, but who knew that at the time? These things are all hindsight.*”

TCL can use hindsight for historical evidence though. Hopefully they’re crunching the numbers…
