# webOS meetup, Chicago, 23rd November

It’s the latest Chicago webOS meetup and it’s happening on Sunday, 23rd November, 7pm at [Moretti’s Ristorante and Pizzeria](http://www.morettischicago.com/schaumburg/index.php), Schamburg.

There’s the latest ‘[Black Eye](http://pivotce.com/2014/11/01/next-luneos-stable-release-available-black-eye/)‘ release of LuneOS to discuss and all the implications off the [HP shutdown](http://pivotce.com/2014/10/16/hp-to-shut-down-catalog-and-cloud-services/). Will there even be time for eating?

Find the details and Sign up [here](http://www.meetup.com/HPwebOS/Chicago/1234603/) or comment on the [webOS Nation thread](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-events/313031-chicago-webos-meetup-2.html#post3425668). Hey, [coupons!](http://www.morettischicago.com/menus/BXTP_COUPN-CATERING_7-14.pdf)

The team at pivotCE remind you that if you are planning a meet up, let us know! We are interested in promoting your events (it might even increase attendance!). We are also interested in reports and pictures from webOS events.

All this free publicity for Moretti’s, but no sign of a donation…
