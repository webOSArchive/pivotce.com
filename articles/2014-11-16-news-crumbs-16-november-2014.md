# News Crumbs 16 November 2014

So much has been happening lately, it’s hard to tell what’s a crumb, crisis or great news! Here is a selection:

- 72ka updates his [Google maps app to 0.3.4](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-development/313394-new-google-maps-webos-native-javascript-api-69.html#post3428125). Check the [webOS Nation App Gallery](http://www.webosnation.com/apps). A Preware feed update is hopefully due soon.
- In his day job as a colour grader, [Project Macaw](http://pivotce.com/2014/01/01/the-eighth-day-of-webos-mas-project-macaw/) developer, [David Cole](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm1084634/?ref_=fn_al_nm_3) has won a [Hollywood Post Alliance](http://hollywoodpostalliance.org/?page_id=21) award for his work on television pilot, “[Reign](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt2710394/?ref_=nm_flmg_vsl_15)“. [Here is a picture of him on stage](http://forums.webosnation.com/redirect-to/?redirect=http%3A%2F%2Fpbs.twimg.com%2Fmedia%2FB14edLWCUAAsuu6.jpg). Congratulations David!
- In other movie news, last week saw the 100th anniversary of the birth of film star [Hedy Lamarr](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001443/?ref_=fn_al_nm_1). [Here’s why she is relevant](http://gizmodo.com/5866310/this-1940s-hollywood-actress-made-wi-fi-happen/1656533867/+knibbs) to this site.
- Another legend! Jason Robitaille, author of Homebrew gateway program, webOS Quick Install, [will be updating it and including the novacom drivers](http://forums.webosnation.com/canuck-coding/274461-webos-quick-install-v4-5-0-a-64.html#post3428572). Jason plans LuneOS support as he awaits delivery of a Nexus 4. Congratulations are also due to Jason for his recent graduation.
- There are problems with Photos posted to Facebook from the webOS app. The problem seems to be at the Facebook end and there’s no solution yet. [Here’s the thread to watch](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-apps-games/328759-facebook-problems.html).
- Forum user, Mazzinia has been scouring the corners of the internet for webOS projects. Here’s some interesting and potentially useful stuff: [Business software](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-apps-games/328895-idempiere-mobile-erp.html#post3428388), [Music](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-apps-games/328900-vkmusic-webos-found-chance.html), [Go agent proxy service](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-apps-games/328909-goagent-free-proxy-service-has-webos-client.html#post3428532), [Python for webOS](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-apps-games/328901-python-webos-again-found-chance.html), [Jumpcore C++ game framework](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-apps-games/328911-jumpcore-2-game-framework-webos-win-linux-osx-iphone-android.html#post3428560), [Bluespice media wiki](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-apps-games/328899-bluespice-mobile-app-webos.html).

With this many crumbs, it’s clear that webOS is very far from being [brown bread](http://www.cockneyrhymingslang.co.uk/slang/brown_bread).
