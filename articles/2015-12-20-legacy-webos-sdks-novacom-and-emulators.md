# Legacy webOS SDKs, Novacom, and Emulators

![early_access_t](../images/files/2015/12/early_access_t.gif)

Yeah yeah it’s almost 2016…why post the SDKs and emulators? Well, if you haven’t noticed people are STILL developing for webOS. And thank God since my Pre3 is humming right along still today. Read on for links and more info.

If you’ve been scouring the net in search of the elusive SDK, novacom, and emulator packages, you may have found [this thread](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-development/325645-looks-like-you-cant-get-sdk-anymore-almost.html) on the webOS Nation forums. Even if you have [your hosts file](http://pivotce.com/2015/06/15/tip-edit-your-hosts-file-for-access-to-old-palm-servers/) modified, the links don’t work. Forum user, bbito, discovered that by [changing the links to http](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-development/325645-looks-like-you-cant-get-sdk-anymore-almost-4.html#post3443899), you can still get the files. But for how long?

I’ve been using my Box account to share as much webOS stuff as I can. I figured adding these files to it and providing a more permanent solution to the issue would be helpful. Hopefully with good links available, this will encourage more devs to dive back in. Links to file from my Box account below! Happy downloading!
