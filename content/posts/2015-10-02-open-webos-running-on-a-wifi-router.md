You just never know where webOS will show up next do you?

A self-described “startup in stealth mode” called [WiFi Hotzones](https://twitter.com/WiFiHotzones) recently posted to Twitter that they’ve got a router running Open webOS.

That’s cool! I like the idea of using my webOS phone, browsing on my webOS tablet, and watching my webOS TV knowing they’re all connected to my home WiFi using a router running webOS!

We (and others) reached out and asked them for proof.

> [@WiFiHotzones](https://twitter.com/WiFiHotzones) whaaaaaat. Shoot us a link or a video or something!
>
> — pivotCE (@pivotCE) [October 1, 2015](https://twitter.com/pivotCE/status/649596552491761665)

To which they replied:

> [@HaDAk](https://twitter.com/HaDAk) We are going to put together a video and screen shots over the next week or two and then release the code!
>
> — WiFi Hotzones (@WiFiHotzones) [October 1, 2015](https://twitter.com/WiFiHotzones/status/649619561378115584)

Awesome! So hold on to your butts because there’s more to come.
