As we mentioned here [back in October](/2014/10/03/lg-to-introduce-a-webos-powered-smart-watch/), LG let slip they were building a webOS smart watch. Thanks to CES, LG has confirmed this to be true. The watch is slated for a 2016 production release.

Android Central has [the exclusive](http://www.androidcentral.com/lg-audi-watch-android-wear-webos) and webOS Nation adds [their own speculation](http://www.webosnation.com/lgs-new-mystery-smartwatch-actually-runs-webos). But the key to take away is that LG is building an ‘ecosystem’. Forgive me for speculating but a company does not build an ecosystem with just a watch and of course, TVs…

So allow me to predict the future for a second: webOS smart phone in 2016. I’m calling it. 😉
