We would like to send our best wishes to all of our readers. 2015 has been a year of ups and downs.

With legacy webOS, a few more things stopped working, a surprising amount got fixed! Though LGwebOS version 3.0 has just been announced, LG have also slimmed down their workforce somewhat and we send extra good will to those who find themselves seeking new challenges: Remember, [LuneOS continues to develop](/2015/12/10/luneos-december-stable-release-caffe-marocchino/) and welcomes anyone who can spare a little time who can help the project along.

Have a great holiday and we shall see what 2016 will bring.
