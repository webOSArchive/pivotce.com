It’s only a few hours left till LG starts its Press Conference on CES 2014.

There were much rumors in the last weeks, but now confirmed from LG it’s no longer a secret that webOS will be the #1 on LG’s next Generation TVs. According to their official newsroom over 70% of the new TV lineup will be powered by our beloved system.

The press conference starts at 8 a.m. PST/4 p.m. GMT/ on Monday, January 6. ~~Take this countdown as a reference:~~

There is official live steam in the LG Newsroom: <http://ces2014.lgnewsroom.com/ces-live/>
