##### These apps were broken on later revisions of webOS:

Mostly because developers never updated them after webOS 2.x or 3.0.5 was released.  They now work on all webOS revisions.

[World Series of Poker: Hold'em Legend](https://appcatalog.webosarchive.org/app/WorldSeriesofPoker:Hold'emLegend)

##### These apps got new features and/or bug fixes:

[Wordsel](https://appcatalog.webosarchive.org/app/Wordsel) - added dark mode

[Sound Cloud Player](https://appcatalog.webosarchive.org/app/SoundCloudPlayer) - volume fix

[webCal Sync](https://appcatalog.webosarchive.org/app/webCalSync) - event duplication fixes

[MLB At Bat 11](https://appcatalog.webosarchive.org/app/MLBAtBat11) - rotation fixes

[Jukie](https://appcatalog.webosarchive.org/app/Jukie) - Sign in fixed, time sync lyrics, and more

[Dead Space](https://appcatalog.webosarchive.org/app/DeadSpace) - Tilt controls fixed

[OTA Ready](https://appcatalog.webosarchive.org/app/OTAReady) - updated for webOS CE 3.2.0 OTA update

[webOS Community Account Manager](https://appcatalog.webosarchive.org/app/webOSCommunityAccountManager)

- added support for webOS 3.2.0 and Lunacy
