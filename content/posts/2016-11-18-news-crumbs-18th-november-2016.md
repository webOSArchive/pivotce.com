Uh, the toaster needs another clean. Let’s shake it before those crumbs are too stale or burnt!

- webOS developer, Choorp either loves twitter or is frightened of it (actually, both is probably normal). He made [this](https://twitter.com/Choorp/status/784866979358380032) based on the logo of premier webOS twitter app, Project Macaw. We salute developer, Penduin for developing [an app](http://preware.pivotce.com/package/net.penduin.halloweenartist) that only gets used once a year.
- [Slashgear reports](http://www.slashgear.com/lg-webos-smart-tvs-gain-online-payment-support-04458631/) that TV’s running LGwebOS 3.0 now have online payment support for shopping & in-app payments. Set those parental controls!
- [TechnoBuffalo](http://www.technobuffalo.com/videos/pal-pre-revisited-video/) is the latest Tech blog looking wistfully back. Are we the only one looking to the future of mobile webOS?
- You want webOS merchandise? [Improvise!](https://twitter.com/2222Davidd/status/788172518247043072)
- Former Palm employee, Lisa Brewster demonstrates [what to do](https://www.instagram.com/p/BLU6l-eDk1R/) if you find yourself in the wild [without a toaster](https://www.instagram.com/p/BLU7OjNjBrM/).

I don’t know about you, but for me, it’s time for [teacakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teacake). See you next time!
