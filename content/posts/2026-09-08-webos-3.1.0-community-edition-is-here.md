While the mobile OS we know and love was a victim of HP's mismanagement, and abandoned by LG as they focused on TVs, the community hasn't given up! Thankfully, HP Open Sourced most of the OS before they called it quits -- a decision which has benefited LuneOS in many ways, and gave us a chance to keep our devices limping along.

Things looked grim as we headed into 2025, but advances in software development brought new hope. Over the past 12 months, a few developers have been able to teach AI coding tools all about our platform -- in exchange, those tools have made it easier to rescue, port, update and fix things.

**webOS 3.1.0** is a culmination of this learning, plus the best of community contributions since the shutdown. As the first complete OS release since 2011, it updates virtually every part of the platform:

- The stock kernel is replaced with **UberKernel** -- proven over more than a decade of overclocking without issues.
- The Launcher is replaced with a new and improved version of **LunaCE**, with icon groups and tab management built-in and all the community-created Tweaks intact.
- The power menu is replaced with **Advanced Reset Options**, now built-in instead of as a Tweak.
- **Exhibition** modes have been improved with new (and persistent!) display options.
- USB device, and Bluetooth Game Controller support are now natively added to the OS.
- Pre-loaded apps and services, including the **App Catalog**, **Maps**, **Backup**, **Help** and **Synergy** have been repaired or replaced with modern equivalents.
- A new **webOS Community Account** enables syncing app data, and enables future community features. Activation is restored -- no more need for deviceTool!
- And most importantly, **TLS 1.3** has been implemented in all key system services, allowing apps and the stock web browser to reach the modern encrypted Internet!

The full [release notes are available on GitHub](https://github.com/webOSArchive/webOS-Community-Edition) -- where you can also find the Release bits.

**webOS 3.1.0** is available as a Doctor which can be used to flash WiFi and 4G full-size TouchPads. 

TouchPad Go's cannot run 3.1.0 but can get most of these updates via new Preware **modernize** feed. See the re-vamped Docs pages for more information: [https://docs.webosarchive.org](https://docs.webosarchive.org)
