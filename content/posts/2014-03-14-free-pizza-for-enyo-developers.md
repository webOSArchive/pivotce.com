There is another meet up in San Francisco. If you are an Enyo developer and in the area on the 26th of March, you can join in! The venue is the LG Silicon Valley Lab. There is pizza on offer [courtesy of Enda McGrath](https://twitter.com/endamcgrath/statuses/443492238673182720), Director of Developer Relations. To me, that sounds like pizza, a chance to play with those new, big, webOS TVs & of course, learning more about [Enyo](http://enyojs.com/) from the experts that built it.

If you can’t make the date or are too far away, the Enyo team are willing to help you organise your own meet ups (according to [Twitter](https://twitter.com/EnyoJS/statuses/444187827077521408)).

I’d write more, but I have a sudden urge to visit my local Italian restaurant. Here is your link: [Let’s talk about Enyo and share some pizza](http://www.meetup.com/Enyo-Meetup/events/170075182/).
