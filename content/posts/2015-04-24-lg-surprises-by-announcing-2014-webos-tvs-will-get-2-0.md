Oh the [wailing and gnashing](http://forums.webosnation.com/lg-webos-tv/328902-new-webos-version-2015-will-not-compatible-current-lg-tvs.html) of teeth that happened [when we found out](http://www.trustedreviews.com/news/existing-webos-tvs-might-not-be-webos-2-0-upgradable) LG’s 2014 webOS TVs wouldn’t be receiving webOS 2.0! So many gnashed teeth…all for naught. [CNet reports](http://www.cnet.com/news/lg-updates-2014-tvs-to-web-os-2-0/) LG will in fact push the 2.0 update out to 2014 webOS TVs.

Ok, it was just a rumor that the sets wouldn’t get the update but still…we don’t need any more bad news!

So everybody calm down. And someone send me a webOS TV. For real.
