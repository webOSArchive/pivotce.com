Derek Anderson is back in Minneapolis for another [Enyo](http://www.enyojs.com) meetup! Following on from an introduction at the start of this month, he will be showing how to set up your development environment, prototype your app and also how to connect it to a public [API](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Api).

Enyo is the cross-platform, javascript framework that enables apps to be developed for [most major platforms](http://enyojs.com/platforms/) including webOS phones, tablets and televisions.

The fun starts at 7pm. More information and registration is [here](http://www.meetup.com/Minneapolis-EnyoJS-Meetup/events/185022212/).
