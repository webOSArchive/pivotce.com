Following up on [webOS 3.1.0 Community Edition](https://www.webosarchive.org/pivot/2026/09/08/webos-3.1.0-community-edition-is-here/), 3.2.0 is the first update available Over-the-Air since at least 2014. This achievement completes the restoration of the platform: every non-commercial component now has a community built and/or hosted replacement. We can fully manage the TouchPad's OS, its services and its apps using Open Source Software.

Of course, this accomplishment is diminished by the aging hardware. If you haven't got a TouchPad, or can't get yours to work, a limited stock of tested units with good batteries is available in the [Store](https://shop.webosarchive.org/) -- get yourself a gadget and help support the archive! 

If you've got one that works, and you've already Doctored it to 3.1.0, you're in for a treat. This update is so much easier:

- Grab [OTA Ready](https://appcatalog.webosarchive.org/app/OTAReady) from the restored App Catalog
- Install it, and confirm the update
- Wait about 10 minutes (be patient) while the 2011-era update process applies 2026-era software bits
- That's it! Enjoy a modernized TouchPad!

This new update finally brings **Emoji** to the platform. Input is achieved with a color Emoji keyboard, while output works by overloading an international font with Black & White glyphs. Apps can choose to substitute those for color icons if they prefer.

![Emoji Keyboard](/uploads/Emoji.png)

The rest of the keyboard has an update as well. The auto-correct engine from 2011 has been replaced with a modern Open Source engine, that hopefully is more accurate and current.

New life is breathed into **Email, Calendar and Contacts**: connect your Gmail, Outlook.com, Exchange, or Microsoft365 accounts, and sync to the built-in apps. While it works well, a few things you should know:

- Outlook.com supports IMAP only, so no Calendar or Contact syncing.
- Microsoft365 is fully verified, but subject to your administrator's approval.
- Gmail is unverified by Google, so will display a warning -- the code is safe and open source, feel free to verify before bypassing Google's scare tactics.
- Contacts can be imported via group vCard, right in the app. Re-importing later won't create duplicates in most cases.
- Calendars can also be imported to the new "On Device" calendar (replaces the HP account), or you can use C+DAV in Preware, or [WebCal Sync](https://appcatalog.webosarchive.org/app/webCalSync) from the App Catalog to sync from standards-based sources.
- The 2011 time zone data has been replaced with a late 2026 set, making international and recurring events a lot more accurate.

The crashy Mojo **Videos** app has been replaced by a stand-alone Enyo/PDK hybrid that handles HTTPS streams gracefully -- even allowing scrubbing.

The **Calculator** now has a scientific mode. Rotate your TouchPad landscape to see the extra keys.

And while its not a functional change, HP has been all but purged from the platform they briefly, but catastrophically mismanaged. The HP boot logo has been replaced with a Luna icon, and various HP strings have been replaced with just webOS. Copyrights are unchanged, and webOS Archive asserts no trademarks -- we're simply trimming the historical references so its clear where this OS came from.

Now that we can OTA, when we find bugs, they can be more easily fixed without hacky patches. Responsible members of the community have the signing keys, and can help maintain the platform going forward.

At this point, though, we're likely nearing the end of this path. Its taken a decade and a half to restore the TouchPad. The Phones are largely casualties of the 3G shut downs happening world-wide, and while there are some modernization updates available in Preware, all of the original hardware is showing its age. Its time for alternative ways to keep the platform alive -- but that's a topic for another post!

For now, learn more about 3.2.0 on GitHub:
[https://github.com/webOSArchive/webOS-Community-Edition](https://github.com/webOSArchive/webOS-Community-Edition)

Then grab the OTA, or Doctor your device fresh, and enjoy the afterlife of webOS!
