There’s not been much popping up from the toaster lately, but there are still a few crumbs to sweep out.

- [gizmo21 reported](http://forums.webosnation.com/lg-webos-tv/328160-avforums-reference-status-award-lg-webos-tv.html#post3421351) that [AVforums](http://www.avforums.com/review/lg-smart-webos-system-review.10451) had awarded LGwebOs reference status – a quality level for other Smart TV platforms to aim at.
- I thought this happened ages ago, but it seems only Marc Aurelian over at [webOS France](http://www.webosfrance.com) was paying attention! [Fin de support de webOS, Blackberry et Symbian par Phonegap](http://www.webosfrance.com/Fin-de-support-de-webOS-Blackberry-et-Symbian-par-Phonegap_a1405.html). The end of phonegap support for webOS, Blackberry & Symbian. (en français, naturellement!). This is not necessarily a huge problem as discussed here: [Phonegap depreciates webOS](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-development/327448-phonegap-depreciates-webos.html).
- [Project Macaw](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-development/320502-project-macaw-update-today.html#post3367645) updated to version 1.5.4. Find it in Preware, but remember if you want to try this Twitter client for the first time, you need to add the special feed…
- LG’s [connect SDK](http://connectsdk.com/download/) has also had a version update. No, it doesn’t [quite](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-development/327774-connect-sdk-here.html#post3417851) work on Legacy webOS, but if you want to make apps that interact with webOS TV’s it’s a must. The new technology in [LuneOS](/2014/06/13/webos-ports-surprises-with-touchpad-port-namelogo-change/) means future support is a possibility…
- Finally, [the conclusion to an odd part of webOS history](http://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2014/07/chubby-checker-hp-settle-penis-size-app-trademark-suit/): The Chubby Checker court case. The comments section of the article contains all the jokes you are likely to need on this matter.

I suddenly fancy a hot muffin…
