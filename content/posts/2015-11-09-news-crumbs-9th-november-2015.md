- [Tomomi Imura](http://www.girliemac.com/index.html), a former developer relations engineer at Palm, now working for Pubnub, showed off a cat facial recognition system on [Leo LaPorte’s Screen Savers](https://twit.tv/shows/new-screen-savers/episodes/19?autostart=false) web show. The segment begins at one hour in, includes some discussion of her time at Palm and lasts about 12 minutes.
- In the [previous Newscrumbs](/2015/08/03/news-crumbs-3rd-august-2015/), we mentioned that Josh Marinacci, former Developer Advocate for webOS at Palm, was looking for work. Good news! He has joined Ms Imura at Pubnub: [The Hubbub About PubNub](http://joshondesign.com/2015/09/26/pubnubhubbub)
- [LG’s lightweight ‘Gram’ laptops arrive in the US](http://www.engadget.com/2015/09/28/lg-gram-laptops-come-to-us/). Who actually owns [gram](http://gr.am)? The lonely little website is still out there if you want the [Prelude font](http://gr.am/css/font-prelude.css)…
