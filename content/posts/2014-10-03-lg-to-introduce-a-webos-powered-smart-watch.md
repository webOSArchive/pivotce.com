Have you been wondering what LG will do with webOS next?

According to [The Verge](http://www.theverge.com/2014/10/2/6888445/lg-webos-smartwatch), a since pulled website has arisen (here are the cached pages from Google [1](http://webcache.googleusercontent.com/search?q=cache:Kl-IF4A-1MgJ:developer.lge.com/webOSSmartWatch/+&cd=1&hl=en&ct=clnk) and [2](http://webcache.googleusercontent.com/search?q=cache:DP_Mecs4Cb4J:developer.lge.com/webOSSmartWatch/common-page/faqs+&cd=2&hl=en&ct=clnk)) detailing info about LG’s all new platform supposedly called “[webOS Wear](http://www.slashgear.com/lg-webos-smartwatch-plans-revealed-02348791/)“. It is thought that this is going to rival Samsung’s Tizen, maybe even in retaliation to Apple’s new smart watch. Hopefully our loyal fan-base will make it popular! According to pictures pulled from the site (see below), the SDK for the said smart watches, will supposedly be released with the announcement of LG’s plans to move webOS to the Smart watch platform.
