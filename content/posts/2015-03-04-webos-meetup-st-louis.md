Mark your calendars for April 3rd, 2015 because we’re doing a webOS Meetup in St. Louis!

Folks are coming from as far as Chicago and Virginia to get to this one and we want to see YOU there too. So hop a train, get in your car, buy a jet, or boat up (or down) the Mississippi River and meet us at Pi Pizza in the Central West End to talk about webOS (and whatever else comes to mind).

webOS Forums user mattmers, is planning on bringing some webOS/Palm sticker sheets and I will personally be bringing one of each webOS devices ever made including a TouchPad Go (no Windsornot, sorry) . Others are bringing rare items like the also unreleased Touchstone 2! There may even be some devices for sale.

**UPDATE**: There is now a GSM Pre3 up for grabs! But you have to be there to win it! More info soon.

**UPDATE 2**: There will be a webOS mystery device there!
