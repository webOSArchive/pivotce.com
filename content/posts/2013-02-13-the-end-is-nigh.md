Things are not well on Planet TouchPad. The universe is bombarding us with destruction and we need all the help we can get!!!

I’m talking about [***Cosmic Nitro***](https://developer.palm.com/appredirect/?packageid=com.galcon.app.cosmicnitro)… It’s old school. It was created for webOS 1.4.5 back in 2009. It was made for the 3.1 inch Pre- screen. *AND IT STILL ROCKS* on TouchPad’s glorious 9.7 inch screen under version 3.x!!!

You’re at the App Catalog: check it out if you haven’t. It’s free, it’s in games. It’s waiting for you to come save planet TouchPad right now!!!

[***Cosmic Nitro***](https://developer.palm.com/appredirect/?packageid=com.galcon.app.cosmicnitro) is deceptively simple, yet highly addictive. Things fall from the sky… Tap on them to blast them before they reach ground level and destroy part of the city. Space ships whiz past and drop bombs… Tap on them quick and blast them out of the sky. Faster and faster the 5 different enemies attack. But you have a secret weapon: swipe the screen to create an energy wave that sweeps upward to destroy them all… Using it sparingly though, you only have a limited amount of pulse power.

9 fun filled levels and an Apocalypse mode with never ending rains of destruction. Pulsing audio with a frantic beat to feed the frenzy you feel as you try to save Planet TouchPad from doom…

The search button is right there in the bottom bar of options… Look for [***Cosmic Nitro***](https://developer.palm.com/appredirect/?packageid=com.galcon.app.cosmicnitro) right now and help us save Planet TouchPad today!!!

Cheers, R
