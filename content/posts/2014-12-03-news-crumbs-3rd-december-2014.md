Ah! There’s nothing like the aroma of freshly baked news to draw the readers in!

- MojoWhatsup [gets an update](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-apps-games/318833-mojowhatsup-whatsapp-client-webos-121.html#post3428800). It seems that Whatsapp will be implementing [end to end security](https://whispersystems.org/blog/whatsapp/). It remains to be seen what impact this will have on the webOS homebrew app.
- If you are trying to convince yourself that you *really need* a new TV this month, then you may be able to reduce any buyer’s remorse by checking [reviewed.com](http://www.reviewed.com/)‘s [list of 2014’s best](http://televisions.reviewed.com/features/2014-best-of-year-television-awards), topped by the 55EA9800 from [LG](http://www.lgnewsroom.com/newsroom/contents/64762) (who also made the runner up).
- As promised, the latest version of [webOS Quick Install](http://forums.webosnation.com/canuck-coding/274461-webos-quick-install-v4-6-0-a-65.html#post3429571) has arrived. Updates for the Preware domain change, Novacom drivers & LuneOS support are all included.
- In a matching update, [Preware feeds have been refreshed](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-development/328732-developers-post-here-if-your-apps-will-available-after-hp-catalogue-closes-4.html#post3429585), so you should now see the apps added to the webOS Nation app gallery over the last few weeks. Automated updates have just been fixed too.
- Looking to the future, take a look at this [prototype app catalogue](http://forums.webosnation.com/general-news-discussion/328780-our-own-app-catalog.html) from forum user and app developer, Warlord9159.
- Finally, don’t panic if you are here looking for the next stable LuneOS build. It has so many major updates that the whole *stability thing* is taking a little longer.

Don’t forget all the above accept donations (A large & mandatory one in the case of the TV). Even pivotCE could do with the occasional crust.
