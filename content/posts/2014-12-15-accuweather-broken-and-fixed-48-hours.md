[Accuweather](http://www.webosnation.com/accuweather) changed their links and discontinued their webOS redirects for the app. The TouchPad app seems largely unaffected but the phones app was rendered broken and bleeding as of a few days ago.

This has been corrected by the community. You can read the start-to-finish [solution](http://forums.webosnation.com/hp-pre-3/329045-accuweather-accuradio-not-working.html) on the forums and then [grab the patch](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-patches/329062-patch-accuweather-connection-fix.html) from the download thread.

Take that, Accuweather!

Unfortunately, Accuradio is suffering from a similar fate and early reports are [not looking good](http://forums.webosnation.com/3430321-post43.html) to whether it can be patched.

Grab the patch and then thank the devs!
