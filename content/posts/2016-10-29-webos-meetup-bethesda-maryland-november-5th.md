Washington, District of Columbia houses the seat of government for the United States of America. Here, many meetings take place and many momentous decisions are made. In November of this year, the citizens of these states face a momentous decision: “Will I attend the webOS meetup in Bethesda?” (Hint: Yes)

That’s right, a few short miles from the White House, Capitol and other notable sites is a branch of [Pi Pizza](http://pi-pizza.com). To be precise, it’s at [7137 Wisconsin Avenue Bethesda, MD 20814.](https://www.google.co.uk/maps/place/Pi+Pizzeria+-+Bethesda/@38.9806646,-77.0921267,21z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0x72c6f4f42abcef92!8m2!3d38.980723!4d-77.092171) Date: November 5th (next Saturday). Time: 6pm.

Here is the [discussion thread](http://forums.webosnation.com/webos-events/327198-any-interest-dc-area-meetup-2.html#post3448866) where you can confirm your attendance.

Bring your webOS and LuneOS devices, discuss developments and the future and exchange tips on keeping your phones and tablets alive and functional.

This meet up is organised by [Alan Morford](/2015/04/16/the-death-and-rebirth-of-a-bass-guitar-case/). Among the devices present will be a [Windsornot](http://www.webosnation.com/windsornot-webos-slate-smartphone-never-was), [TouchStone 2](http://www.webosnation.com/review-hp-touchstone-2-audio-dock) and a 64GB [TouchPad Go](http://www.webosnation.com/review-hp-touchpad-go).

The team at pivotCE remind you that if you are planning a meet up, let us know! We are interested in promoting your events (it might even increase attendance!). We are also interested in reports and pictures from webOS events.
